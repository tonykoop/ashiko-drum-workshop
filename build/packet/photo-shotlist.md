## Priority re-shoots or new shots needed

### P1 — Hero product shot (priority: HIGH)
A clean three-quarter view of a finished ashiko showing: shell grain, head, and rope lacing.
Current `images/hero-render.png` is a concept render; a real workshop drum photo would be preferred.
Setup: neutral background, side lighting to show grain texture, head tension visible.

### P2 — Stave cross-section close-up
Macro of assembled stave joint at the shell exterior: shows tight glue-line and grain direction.
Supplement to the stave dimension drawing (D1).

### P3 — Sled jig in use (priority: MEDIUM)
Table saw with compound-miter sled in position, stave blank loaded, about to cut.
Documents the sled correctly positioned and operator technique.

### P4 — Lathe fixture mounted
Keyed shaft engaged in lathe headstock, drum body mounted, ready for turning pass.
Confirms correct fixture orientation.

### P5 — Finished drum family shot (priority: MEDIUM)
Multiple finished drums from the workshop together — showing wood variety (cherry, maple, walnut).
Currently no clean multi-drum family photo exists; the glue-up photos show multiple shells.

### P6 — Head mounting sequence (priority: LOW)
Wet goatskin over hoop, rope lacing start, first tension pass, final tuned head.
Adds build documentation for the heading stages currently undocumented photographically.
