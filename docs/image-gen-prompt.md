# Image-Gen Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

A finished traditional West African ashiko hand drum standing upright as a real physical object: a tall truncated-cone shell, wide at the goatskin head and tapering to a narrow open foot, built from eighteen vertical hardwood staves glued into a seamless conical body. The staved construction reads clearly through the finish — alternating warm tones of cherry, hard maple, deep red padauk, and violet-brown purpleheart running as long vertical strips up the cone, lacquered to a soft satin sheen that catches the light. A single hairless goatskin head is stretched taut across the wide top, laced down with fine cream double-braided polyester rope in a vertical V-pattern of pulls running around the upper shell, gathered into a tuning weave near the rim. Realistic drum scale, roughly knee-to-thigh height, sitting on a clean neutral light-gray studio sweep. Soft directional studio light from upper left, gentle falloff, 3/4 product-photography angle, ~50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic, no text labels.

## In-Context Prompt

The same staved hardwood ashiko drum staged in a warm makerspace woodshop: tilted slightly forward and held between the knees of a seated player in the West African playing posture, hands resting on the goatskin head mid-strike. Background shows soft out-of-focus shop detail — a wood lathe, ratchet straps and clamps on a bench, several other finished multi-wood drum bodies waiting in a row — telling the story of a group build. Natural window light raking across the rope lacing and the vertical grain of the staves, shallow depth of field, environmental storytelling, concept visualization only.

## Detail Prompt

Macro close-up of the upper shell where the goatskin head meets the staved hardwood body: the taut translucent goatskin lapped over the rim, the fine cream double-braided polyester rope drawn into tight diagonal pulls and a tuning weave, and the precise glue seams between adjacent cherry, maple, padauk, and purpleheart staves visible just below as crisp vertical joint lines. Raking low-angle light emphasizing the rope texture, the skin grain, and the satin-lacquered wood surface, high detail, no measurements.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, or fake maker marks; no dimension callouts or measurement lines; no impossible rope routing or floating hardware; anatomically plausible hands if a player is shown.
