# Ashiko Drum Workshop Image-Gen Prompts

Concept visualization only; not fabrication authority. Do not infer stave
angles, fixture dimensions, head sizes, or rope lengths from these prompts.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 product photograph of a finished stave-built ashiko hand drum from the workshop archive: a tall tapered hardwood shell made from many vertical staves in cherry, hard maple, padauk, and purpleheart tones, lathe-turned smooth, finished in clear lacquer, with a natural goatskin head and taut polyester rope tuning around the rim. Show the conical ashiko silhouette, crisp stave seams, clean bearing edge, and workshop-made character, with a soft hand-drum mallet or table-saw sled off to one side for scale. Clean studio background, soft directional lighting, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic makerspace workshop scene from an ashiko-building class: several partially finished stave shells on benches, labeled staves, ratchet straps or rope clamps, a table-saw sled nearby, and one finished ashiko being tuned with goatskin and rope. People may be present as careful adult builders, but hands must look natural and safety-aware. Warm shop light, sawdust, clamps, lathes and jigs in the background, documentary realism.

## Detail / Macro Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of the ashiko's construction signature: alternating hardwood staves meeting in clean vertical seams, the shell curve transitioning into a smooth bearing edge, goatskin folded over the rim, and braided rope crossing through the tuning pattern. Show lacquer sheen, wood grain, small handmade variation, and real tension in the rope. High-detail macro, shallow depth of field.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, impossible rope paths, unsafe tool use, extra limbs, distorted hands, fake labels, or culturally generic fantasy decoration.
